Research checked **15 October 2026**. This compares relevant workflows, not company valuations or
an audited product ranking. Vendor descriptions are evidence of advertised capabilities, not independent
proof of accuracy, outcomes or superiority. No product accounts were purchased or field-tested.

## Evidence standards to learn from

[UN-Habitat's Waste Wise Cities Tool](https://unhabitat.org/wwc-tool) separates generation, collection
and management in controlled facilities. Taka Sats should not turn recorded intake into a recycled-mass
claim. [Verra's Plastic Program](https://verra.org/programs/plastic-waste-reduction-standard/) includes
independent audit, quantification methodologies and registry controls. Software output is an input to
such work, not a replacement or an automatic credit.

## Strategic choice

Focus on **a community-owned work record with evidence, fair payment controls and a portable portfolio**.
Do not claim unique technology: offline forms, traceability, incentives and cryptographic records already
exist elsewhere. The opportunity is a useful combination that groups can inspect, operate and adapt
without a franchise or central service requirement. Validate that opportunity with a second independent
operator and actual report users before expanding into credits, route planning or global rankings.
